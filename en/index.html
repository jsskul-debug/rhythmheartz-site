FAQ

Good to know

I've never danced before. Is that OK?

Yes. Every class starts with a warm-up and basic moves before the choreography.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and any sneakers, including the ones you walk in. No need to bring a spare pair. Bring water.

What is the cancellation policy?

Group Class: let us know at least 24 hours before. Private Class: full deposit refund if you cancel 24+ hours before class; no refund for later cancellations or no-shows.

What if the Group Class doesn't reach 3 people?

We contact you 2 days before to offer another date, a Private Class, or a free cancellation.
